# TestVeh / Vehicle Identity OS — Creative Direction 01

## Product truth

The original public portfolio/profile describes TestVeh as an early Django/React authentication prototype extended into vehicle-record and registration-review work. This direction preserves that use case. The `platform/` booking prototype is deliberately not presented as a built-in feature of the registry.

**Positioning:** an experimental vehicle *identity interface*, not an auto-commerce homepage. Make the act of understanding a vehicle feel tactile, legible and trust-aware.

## Visual language

- **Midnight pine** (`#0D1D1E`): the engineering terminal and technical cutaway stage.
- **Signal lime** (`#CEEF8A`): active selections, primary actions, navigational emphasis and measurement accents.
- **Specimen paper** (`#EEE9DD`): vehicle identity dossier that resembles a physical technical archive.
- **Coral safety** (`#FF8E6D`): specimen emphasis without implying verified approval.
- **Typography:** black-weight condensed-feeling editorial headlines paired with compact monospace reference identifiers and careful body text.
- **Motion:** a slow scanning line provides atmosphere only, never an actual diagnostic claim; disabled for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Interaction metaphor

1. **Object:** study the original technical vehicle illustration.
2. **Identity:** choose from three clearly fictional local vehicle files. Selection updates the sidebar model, plate, fuel, location, review state, inspection and document completeness.
3. **Dossier:** open the same vehicle in a full detail dialog.
4. **Register:** search, filter, create, revise, inspect and export local records.
5. **Honesty:** show data ownership and demo limits beside the work, not hidden in the footer.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop uses a two-column engineering stage and specimen sheet. At narrow widths, they stack into one reading flow, without shrinking reference text to unusable sizes. Dense tabular data scrolls inside its own pane.

## Safety boundaries

Never visually represent a local 'Reviewed (demo)' state as official certification. The passport stamp says DEMO RECORD ONLY, the document-check caption states that checks are self-recorded, and the service-wide disclaimer states there is no government connection. Avoid real identities and registration documents in examples.

## Future creative extensions

- Time-travel view: show dated, recorded changes with true audit provenance once a server-backed event history exists.
- Inspection theatre: visualize checklist completion and due-date risk while distinguishing operator-entered observations from evidence-backed confirmations.
- Multi-vehicle atlas: compare dossier completeness with clear, user-controlled visual filters.
- Only later: verified document uploads, permissioned collaboration, and audit trails, contingent on infrastructure and privacy reviews.

This document describes intentional UI behavior and future design paths, not implemented regulatory verification.
